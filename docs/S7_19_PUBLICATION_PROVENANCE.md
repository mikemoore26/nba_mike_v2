# S7.19 — Historical publication provenance

## Decision

Keep `RESEARCH_ONLY / BLOCK_TRAINING` for all five injury reports. S7.18 extraction accuracy and coverage do not prove historical availability. The PDFs examined in the research environment have no populated creation/modification timestamps, and such timestamps would not establish public availability even if present.

## Audit scope

SHA256 identities, PDF validity, page counts, raw PDF creation/modification metadata, and a non-promoting evidence manifest. No predictions, model training, source data modification, or internet retrieval.

## Future promotion gate

Require an independent, authenticated historical capture (or equivalent contemporaneous source) identifying the report or exact content, an evidence timestamp earlier than the applicable prediction cutoff, and a review of report revisions. Evaluate each report and each cutoff independently. A present-day download cannot establish March 2026 publication timing.

## Other pending gate

Team/matchup and inherited-context assignments still need independent verification; do not conflate that with publication provenance.
