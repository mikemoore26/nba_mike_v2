# S7.49 — Archived claim audit

This offline step rechecks SHA-256 object integrity and requires a grammatical transaction-direction pattern rather than mere player/team/action co-occurrence. It classifies reported/agreed, pending-approval and announcement language without treating any as confirmed completed events. It explicitly flags missing player/team mapping (including Anthony Davis) and does not infer source independence from multiple archived copies of the same article.

## Limitations

S7.48 best excerpts are truncated to 450 characters and may come from structured metadata or archive replay. The archival capture timestamp has not been independently attested. Original page URL and local SHA integrity do not establish the original publication time. `RESEARCH_ONLY / BLOCK_TRAINING` is mandatory.
