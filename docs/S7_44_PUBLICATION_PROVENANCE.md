# S7.44 — Historical publication provenance gate

S7.44 inventories HTML meta tags, JSON-LD dates, and `<time>` elements from existing SHA-256-verified official NBA article snapshots. It deliberately distinguishes publication *candidates* from independently proven publication timestamps. Multiple dates can refer to original publication, modification, or unrelated page content. Article publication timing is distinct from a player's transaction date, and current downloaded HTML does not prove that the article was available at an earlier cutoff.

No event identity, historical availability, or source independence is promoted. `RESEARCH_ONLY / BLOCK_TRAINING`.
