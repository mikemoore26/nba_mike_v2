# S7.48 — Archived content recovery

Offline, hash-checked extraction of HTML title, meta title/description, JSON-LD, heading and paragraph fields from S7.46 archived objects. Compares each local excerpt to the S7.47 player, origin and destination. Flags URL-only player mentions separately from archived content. Output evidence remains heuristic, including multi-team syntax, archived replay contamination, syndication and historical capture provenance. No promotion to S7.31/S7.26/S7.23 or model training.
